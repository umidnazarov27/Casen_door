# Image sizes for the kitchen page

Prepared 8 November 2026 for William. On 8 November the client asked for image frames to be sized for the website rather than fitted to the supplied photos; the client will adjust the images later. The page now uses fixed frames: every photo is cropped to fill its frame instead of shrinking to fit.

## What to supply

| Image | Shape | Export size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Layouts, islands, carcass finishes, storage options | Square (1:1) | 2000 × 2000 px | One photo per option. The same file is used in several frames (below), so keep the subject in the centre. |
| Completed projects ("Previous work") | Landscape 5:4 | 1600 × 1280 px | Two photos, each with a short caption. |
| Full-kitchen combination photos | Square (1:1) | 2000 × 2000 px | One per combination listed in `output/photo-coverage/…/photo-checklist.csv`. |

For every image: JPEG or WebP, sRGB colour, under about 500 KB after compression, named by the option ID (for example `carcass-oak.jpg`).

## Where each option photo appears

One square master photo is cropped to each of these frames. Sizes are measured on a 1440 px wide desktop screen; phones show smaller frames.

| Frame | Shape | Shown size | What is cropped from a square photo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main gallery image | 1:1 | 681 × 681 px | Nothing |
| Option list thumbnail | 1:1 | 88 × 88 px | Nothing |
| Feature circles ("The details make the kitchen") | Circle | 205 px | Corners |
| Layout cards (step 1) | 1.65:1 | 248 × 150 px | About 20% from the top and from the bottom |
| Gallery thumbnails | 1.76:1 | 88 × 50 px | About 22% from the top and from the bottom |
| Finish grid ("Find your finish") | 3:2 | 302 × 201 px | About 17% from the top and from the bottom |
| Benefits ("Why invest in a kitchen?") | 5:4 | 657 × 525 px | 10% from the top and from the bottom |

**Safe area:** keep the important part of each option photo inside the middle 55% of its height. Then no frame cuts it off.

The enlarged view (the ↗ button) always shows the whole photo without cropping.

## Current photos compared with the guide

The 14 photos from 3 November are not square, so the page currently crops them:

- Carcass finishes (1536 × 1024, landscape): the sides are cropped in the square frames.
- Storage options (portrait, 227–756 px wide): the top and bottom are cropped, and they are smaller than 2000 px. The tall pull-out (227 × 324) is the smallest.
- Layouts and islands (1254–1600 px square): already the right shape; a 2000 px version would look sharper on high-resolution screens.

The client said on 8 November that resolution does not need attention yet, so none of this blocks the current work.
